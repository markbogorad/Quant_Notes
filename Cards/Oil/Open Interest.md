# Open Interest
- Amount of futures not closed or settled (futures outstanding in the market)
- Think of it as ongoing trades
- **High OI** → More active market, easier to enter/exit positions.
- **Falling OI** → Positions are closing out, less liquidity.
- **Rising OI with price increases** → Strong trend confirmation.
- **Rising OI with price drops** → Strong selling pressure.
