# Feedback Loop in Oil
- Oil operates in a massive system of bi directional causality
- **Negative** (balancing) loops are associated with [[Mean Reversion Process (Ornstein-Uhlenbeck)]]
	- Examples:
		- Oil goes up, U.S. inflation goes up (gasoline explains 75% of variance), impacts monetary policy (raise rates), capital flows where interest rate is higher, boosts U.S. dollars, oil is denominated by more and is pressured down by higher dollar
		- Safe haven loop: people flock from equities and etc to U.S. treasuries, dollar becomes stronger
		- Occasionally, oil itself can become a safe haven -> correlation between USD and oil becomes positive here!
- **Positive** (reinforcing) loops are associated with [[Oil Momentum Risk Premia]]
	- Examples:
		- Oil goes up, EM markets grow, money flocks to EMs, dollar falls, inflation goes up, higher demand for oil, higher oil price
