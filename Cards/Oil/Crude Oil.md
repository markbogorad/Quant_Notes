# Crude Oil
- Crude oil is essentially the main thing that is produced
	- Trades in barrels (1 barrels = 42 gallons)
	- 1 future = 1,000 barrels = 42,000 gallons
- Sub products are refined ([[Oil Refining]])
	- Petroleum complex
		- Jet Fuel
		- Distillate 
			- Diesel and heating oil
			- Have a diversified usage
		- Gasoline
			- engine fuel for cars
			- RBOB (87 octane gasoline)
				- To get higher octane, you add ethanol - sold on [[Physical Trading]] marketplace or 
- Barrel is sorted by weight (gasoline is the lightest)

- Crude oil has some direct usage other than being the source of the refined products
	- Burning crude oil can directly make electricity (power plants)
	- Heavy crudes used in asphalt production
