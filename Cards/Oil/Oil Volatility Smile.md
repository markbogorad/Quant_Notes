# Oil Volatility Smile
- Models [[Implied Volatility]]
- Has a negative (put) skew due to lognormal [[Asymmetry of Black-Scholes]]
	- Underpricing the puts
- Compensating for lack of left side probability mass in the lognormal distribution\
	- Artificially increasing plug factor to match market
- High implied vol tells you how wrong your model is (not enough on the left)
