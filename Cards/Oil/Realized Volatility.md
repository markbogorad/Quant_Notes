# Realized Volatility
- Single realization of an unobservable stochastic process
- Backward looking statistic -> volatility of futures returns
- In commodities, they prefer measuring std of price changes (not returns) because they are normal and it makes sense since your underlying is futures (in dollars per barrel per unit of time)
	- This measure is normal, not lognormal
