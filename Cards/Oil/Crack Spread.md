# Crack Spreads
- The spread between crude oil and it's refined products
	- Can be priced with [[Real Options]]
## Conversions
- Ex: RBOB WTI spread must be converted to barrels
$$\text{RBOB Crack} = RBOB * 42 - WTI$$
- 2-1-1 basket spread (RBOB and heating oil as a refining margin proxy)
$$margin = (HO * 42) + (RBOB * 42) - (2 * WTI)$$
