# Volatility Term Structure
- Samuelson Effect - volatility should naturally decline for longer maturity futures (short term effects are smoothed out by balancing mechanism of storage [[Dynamic Theory of Storage (Price-Inventory Feedback Loop)]])
	- With enough time, supply and demand will normalize because of storage
- High vol would come from falling prices - steepening of curve
- Low vol - flattening of curve
