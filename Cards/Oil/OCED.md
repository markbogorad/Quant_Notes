# Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)
- A term used to differentiate developed economies and emerging markets, typically in oil context
	- Term is politicized - basically the west vs BRICS
- Developed economies: United States, Canada, Europe, Japan, South Korea, and Australia.
- Emerging economies: China, India, Brazil, and much of Africa and Southeast Asia.
