# Oil Curve Convexity
- Essentially [[Oil Carry Risk Premia]] applied to [[Time (Calendar) Spreads]]
	- Convexity as an indicator (2nd order derivative of the spread) can have useful information on directionality
		- The convexity of the curve will dictate optimal trading
- *Shape of the futures curve reflects storage cost*
- Extreme versions of [[Contango & Backwardation Markets]] can affect trading strategy dynamics, on particular the [[Oil Carry Risk Premia]] which is contingent upon rolling along the curve

## Convexity Practicality
- Reconstruct the futures curve for M1 M2 spreads
![[Pasted image 20250224091017.png]]
- Spreads decreasing -> flattening of the curve
- **Strategy: sell steeper, shorter T spread and buy the longer one as a hedge**
	- Concave: sell the spread
		- Gravity will cause it to roll down steeper
	- Convex: buy the spread
		- Gravity will cause it to roll up steeper
	- Works as a butterfly (difference of spreads)
- Essentially [[Oil Carry Risk Premia]] applied to [[Time (Calendar) Spreads]]
	- Taking the 2nd derivative (difference of spreads) but trading the 1st derivative (carry)
- In interviews - what is the carry of the carry/what is the spread of the spread - asking you about the convexity of the curve
- curve trading
	- shape of curve usually stays but you're rolling futs up or down the curve
