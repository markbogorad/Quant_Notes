# Canonical Theory of Storage
- Working curve theory
	- **Theory: the shape of the futures curve is driven by inventories**
		- Low inventories = high [[Convenience Yield]] = backwardation ([[Contango & Backwardation Markets]])
		- High inventories = low convenience yield = contango
	- Looks at the operator of storage facilities as an enterprise where marginal profit should equal marginal cost
	$$F-S=U+R-Y$$
		- Marginal cost (forward value above spot) = marginal profit (storage + rate - convenience yield)
	- ![[Pasted image 20250211140631.png]]
		- Left side shows low supply -> as we get lower supply we are willing to pay more for the commodity NOW relative to futures
		- Middle of the curve essentially reflects cost of storage
		- If storage gets very full -> spot price drops rapidly
- Considers boundary effects 
	- Bottom: zero inventory
		- The closer you are to this, the higher the [[Convenience Yield]]
	- Top: constraint on storage
