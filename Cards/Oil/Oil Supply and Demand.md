# Behavior of Oil Supply and Demand
- Both supply and demand are very hard to derive
	- Supply is a national secret for many countries and data can be biased
	- Demand is hard to gauge - people actually typically approximate it by evaluating supply - change in inventory
- Oil supply = production + imports
- Oil demand = consumption + exports
	- Oil demand roughly: 100 million barrels per day
	- we trade 6 billion per day (60X consumption)
- Supply and demand aren't very observable but inventories are
- Inventories are the most important variable to track
## Inelasticity Contributing to Volatility
- Supply and demand are extremely inelastic
	- Supply: long lag between discovery of oil and production (except shale which is fast)
	- Demand: consumption habits don't change - people pay higher price for oil because its necessary
		- Price elasticity of demand $e = \frac{dD/D}{dP/P} = -0.05$ roughly
