# Early Expiry Options
- Short term option on long term future -> decoupled from it's future
- Good for producers
- Good for betting on an event 
## Working with time dependent volatility
- [[Time Dependent Local Volatility]]
- You average the volatility with respect to the time of the option, not the future
	- These are overall less volatile because you chop off the highest volatility bit at near expiry
- DO NOT USE 1 NOR 2 YEAR VOL -> need to back out early expiry vol (14.8%)
