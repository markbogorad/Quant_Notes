# Inflation Hedgers
- Causes risk premium to become negative (caused [[Theory of Normal Contango]])
	- Inflation hedgers are largest in market (bigger than producer)
	- Other side (sellers) are inventory hedgers (storage people who can store oil)
- These came up after financialization of oil circa 2004
	- Oil became its own strategic asset class as a proxy for commodities, Goldman made GSCI, etc..
- **GSCI brought real money, [[Risk Parity (All Weather)]] and inflation hedgers brought leverage**
