# Volatility Trading
- Goal is not to predict direction
- The goal is to make small money on the day of the trade and maintain the profit by hedging
	- Hedging can bleed away profits

## Behavior of Oil Volatility
- Reason for oil volatility
	- Supply and demand are extremely inelastic
		- Supply: long lag between discovery of oil and production (except shale which is fast)
		- Demand: consumption habits don't change - people pay higher price for oil because its necessary
