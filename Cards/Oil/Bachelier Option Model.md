# Bachelier Model
- Invented in 1900, 76 years before Black-Scholes
- Basically [[Black-Scholes]] but with normal distribution
- A pioneer of [[Stochastic Calculus MOC]]
- Allows prices to go negative
- Normally distirbuted
- Constant volatility and local volatility
- Symmetric [[Delta]]
