# Gabillon Model (1991)
$$F(t,T)=St​exp(A(τ)+B(τ)ln(St​))$$
- A simpler alternative to the [[Two Factor Stochastic Convenience Yield Model (Oil)]]
- Captures [[Mean Reversion Process (Ornstein-Uhlenbeck)]] and Samuelson effect in volatility (term structure)
	- No stochastic [[Convenience Yield]] or correlation
- **Gabillon = Simplified Mean-Reverting Model** for the log spot price, with no convenience yield.
- **Schwartz = Fully Equipped**, with a stochastic convenience yield, allowing for a richer and more realistic description of futures prices.
