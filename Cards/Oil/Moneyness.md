# Moneyness
- Indicates whether a money has intrinsic value if exercised immediately - **captures likelihood of exercise**
### Log Moneyness
$$ln(\frac{S}{K})$$
- Positive - ITM
- 0 - ATM
- Negative - OTM
### Strike Moneyness
- Traditional most simple definition to see if you're in the money
- Call: $S>K$
- Put: $S<K$
### [[Delta]] Moneyness
- Basically using delta as a marker for being ITM or OTM
- Calls: $\Delta > 0.50$
- Puts $\Delta < -0.50$
