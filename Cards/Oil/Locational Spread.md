# Locational Spread Option
- An option on the difference of oil prices in 2 locations
- Oil pipelines are valued as a [[Real Options]] in this context
	- Ships are [[Real Options]] between WTI and Brent - [[WTI-Brent Accordion]]
	- These types of option arise very often, almost at every step of the supply chain
- **Strategy:** any time the price differential of the two commodities of interest exceeds cost of shipping, you sell the expensive one and buy the cheap one
	- Like a call option on a spread
