# Oil Market Participants
- [[OPEC+]]
	- Works kind of like the central bank - their decision to cut or increase supply will move the market
		- Kind of like supply of money and interest rates
- Producers
	- **[[Real Options]]: Production**
		- Long call on crude oil
	- National oil companies
	- US Independents (Devon Energy, Occidental Petroleum, Pioneer Natural Resources, EOG Resources, and ConocoPhillips)
		- Exploration and extraction of oil
- Trading Houses (storage Hedgers)
	- **[[Real Options]]: storage**
		- the strongest [[Real Options]] in practice
	- Largest party in the participants ecosystem
	- Vitol, Trafigura, Gunvor, Glencore
- Energy Majors (Refineries)
	- ExxonMobil, Chevron, Valero
		- Don't hedge as much because they can naturally diversify with [[Oil Refining]]
			- Ex: when oil price is cheap, production suffers, but refining does very well
	- [[Oil Refining]]
- US Independents
	- Required to hedge by banks due to low capital ([[Oil Options]])
	- Highly leveraged
