# Normal Backwardation
- [[Convenience Yield]] > storage cost
- This is not the same concept as [[Contango & Backwardation Markets]] 
	- normal backwardation can be present in both backwardation and contango and refers to the **risk premium**
		- Market backwardation is a statement only on the shape of futures
		- Normal backwardation is a risk premium theory that says **futures are biased downward to expected future spot**
			- **[[Roll Yield]] (Roll Return is positive)**
			- **Market for hedging services is in natural disequilibrium** because speculators demand compensation for taking on the hedgers risk (Keynes showed that agricultural commods made up 1.5% of GDP but 50% vol)
- By Keynes and held from 1983-2004
	- Argued the market should be in disequilibrium - producers should attract hedgers (speculators) and they must be incentivized by selling futures cheaper than fair market value. 
	- Due to a natural structural imbalance in commodities. 
	- Futures are not a [[Martingales]] here and should constantly trade below spot. 
	- The speculator is entering the trade with the intention to make money
		- Producers are stuck and have to commit to this - can't switch out of owning a refinery
		- Consumers are are more free - e.g. an switch to bikes

- This does NOT involve expectation
	- Traditional [[Contango & Backwardation Markets]] is based on expectation
- 2 main conclusions
	- Producers are naturally short futures
	- To attract speculators, futures are **set below expected spot price**, offering a **risk premium**
## Risk Premium under Normal Backwardation
$$RP = E_t(S(T)) - S(t) + (S(t) - F(t,T))$$
- Risk premium = expected change in the spot price + the [[Oil Own Rate]] (extrapolated from futures)
- In normal backwardation, risk premium is **positive**
- Market backwardation vs normal backwardation
	- Market - simply refers to shape of futures curve
	- Normal backwardation - refers to difference between spot and futures (grey shaded area) 
		- Expected spot price is decreasing
