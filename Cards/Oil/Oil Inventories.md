# Inventories
- Oil inventories = supply - demand
	- Supply = production + imports
	- Demand = consumption + exports
	- [[Oil Supply and Demand]]
- Change in inventory is the only true observable variable
- Inventories change by the amount of supply and demand:
$$x(t) - x(t-dt) = Z(t)- D(t)$$
- X(t) - inventories
- Z(t) - supply
- D(t) - demand

- Accumulated inventories are bounded [[Stylized Model of the Squeeze]]
$$0<X_{min}<x(T)=\int_t^T(Z(t)-D(t))dt\leq X_{max}$$
- X max happened during [[Negative Oil Prices]]
- Storage balancing is the long term [[Mean Reversion Process (Ornstein-Uhlenbeck)]] valve
