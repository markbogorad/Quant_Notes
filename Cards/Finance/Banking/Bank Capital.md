# Capital
- Essentially a buffer for banks to tanks [[Market Risk]] and [[Credit Risk]]
	- Enough assets and assets of good quality to not enter [[Insolvency]]
- Regulators and companies don't view capital the same - close but do not match
## Tiers of Capital
- Capital tiers (ranked by liquidity where more liquid is best quality - considers how long you can hold the money for as well with subordinated debt types)
	- Tier I
		- Common stock and retained earnings
	- Tier II
		- Supplementary bank capital, hybrid instruments (can turn part of it into money), revaluation reserves, undisclosed reserves, subordinated term debt (cant be called on you immidieately so usable money)
		- Less liquid funds that can be turned into money fairly easy
	- Tier III
		- Less liquid stuff
## Capital Ratios
- Regulatory capital
	- $\frac{RC}{RWA_M+RWA_C+RWA_O} \geq$ X%
	- RC is regulatory capital
	- M - Market risk
	- C - credit risk
	- O - operational risk
	- X was 8% in [[Basel II]] and 10.5% in [[Basel III]]
- Tier 1 for GSIBs
	- 5 in US, 2 in UK
	- These banks have a very vast client range
