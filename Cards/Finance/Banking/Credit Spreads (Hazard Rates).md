# Credit Spreads (CS01)
- The risk premium of credit
	- Proxy for [[Credit Risk]]
- Difference between the yield of a **corporate bond** and the yield of a **risk free asset**
$$ = Yield_{x}-Yield_{rf}$$
- **Higher Credit Spread** → Indicates greater perceived credit risk. Investors demand a higher return for holding riskier debt.
- **Lower Credit Spread** → Suggests lower perceived risk and higher confidence in the issuer’s ability to repay.
- Downgrading of credit rating increases credit spread
## CS01 - Credit Sensitivity (Spread Duration)
$$\frac{\Delta Price}{\Delta Spread}$$
- $\Delta Spread$ is usually 1 bps
- Ex: $CS01=50$ means for every 1 bps change in the spread, your bond will decrease by $50
