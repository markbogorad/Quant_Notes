# Natural Gas
- Primarily methane ($CH_4$)
	- If it has ethane, propane, or pentane liquids it is more rich (aka more wet)
- 27% of energy in USA
- Can be cooled to a liquid and transported in trucks (liquified natural gas aka LNG)
- Benchmark natural gas hub: Henry Hub in Louisiana --> reflects US industry as a whole
- Used for electricity (40% of electricity comes from natgas)
	- Also for manufacturing and heating
	- Houston is a gas place
- Very seasonal
- Trades in MMBTU
	- 1 oil barrel = 5.8 MMBTUs, 1 MMBTU = 1,000 cubic feet
	- 1 futures = 10,000 MMBTUs
- Expensive to ship - must cooled to liquid, transported in tankers, then re gasified
