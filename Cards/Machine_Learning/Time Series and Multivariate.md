### Multivariate
- Identifying relationships between multiple variables
- [[Correlation]], [[Covariance]], [[Dimensionality Reduction]]
	- [[PCA]]
- Factor Analysis: A method to uncover latent factors that explain observed correlations among variables.
- Canonical Correlation Analysis: A method to understand the relationships between two sets of variables.
- [[Clustering]] Analysis: Classifies objects into groups based on the similarity of attributes (e.g., [[K-Means Clustering]], [[Hierarchical Clustering Analysis (HCA)]]).
- Discriminant Analysis: A classification technique to predict categorical dependent variable with multiple independent variables.
- Multivariate Analysis of Variance: Extends ANOVA to situations with multiple dependent variables.
- Partial Least Squares Regression: Regression that handles cases where predictors highly collinear, used when number of predictors > observations.
