# 2-D [[Heat Equation]]
- Application in finance: [[Quanto Options]] - options in 2 different currencies
- Describes how heat diffuses over time in a two-dimensional region
$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \alpha \left( \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} \right)$$
- Initial condition: $u(x, y, 0) = f(x, y)$
