# Separation of Variables
- A common solution to linear [[PDE]]
- The method assumes that the solution V(S,t) can be written as a product of two functions—each depending on a single variable: $V(S,t)=X(S)T(t)$
- Where:
	- $X(S)$ is a function of $S$ (the stock price)
	- $T(t)$ is a function of $t$ (time)
- The goal is to separate the equation so that each variable (stock price and time) is handled independently, turning the PDE into 2 simpler [[ODE]]
	- Solve each ODE and combine the answers
