# Picard Existence Theorem
$$\frac{dx}{dt} = f(x, t), \quad x(0) = x_0$$
- Provides a theoretical guarantee for the existence and uniqueness of solutions to [[ODE]]s under certain conditions.
- **Key Idea**: If the function $f(x,y)$ is continuous and satisfies a [[Lipschitz Condition]] in y, then a unique solution exists in a neighborhood of the initial condition.
