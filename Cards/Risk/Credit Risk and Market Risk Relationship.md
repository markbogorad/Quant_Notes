# Credit Risk & Market Risk
- Both require knowledge on the behavior of some default component (lambda vs _)
- Credit risk

	- CDS
		- Priced to 0
		- Paying fixed coupon for a default return (1- recovery amount)
		- Formula for CDS price is like principal payment formula except youre on the other side of the transaction (hence -) and your C is (1-r)
	- Credit risks are larger (accumulate over longer horizons)
		- Billions vs 10s of millions
	- Credit risk is related to portfolio concentration via counterparty risk
		- Ex: if all counterparties come from the same country, same sector, etc
	- Credit risk losses cluster
	- Credit risk is harder to recover from
