# Backtesting VaR
- Backtesting
	- **VaR is 1 day behind PnL (very important)**
		- VaR is a forward measure trying to predict loss
- Purpose of backtesting - a reality check on how accurate your VaR has been
- Standard charges vs model approach is by line of business then by desk level (have to put risky desks into standard charges)
	- The more times the backtest is breached 0 the closer you get to getting forced to do standard charges by [[Basel Regulation]]
