# Risk Management
- Do we know what bets we are making? Are we aware of all of the right risks? Were positions within limits? Is this a bet we can afford to make?
- Risk management should never take ownership of PnL, report to a business, be responsible for pricing, or create their own risks
## Introductory Lecture
 - Risk management systems in practice are optimized for traders
	- Often times very poorly made and managed (bad IT teams in hedge funds and banks)
	- Systems aren't calculated the same way by all parties in the fund/bank
	- A lot of risk managers are often data managers because the systems are so bad
- Derivatives risk
	- Risk of derivative movement + counterparty
		- Counterparty goes to CVA desk
		- CVA owns that P&L -> they get screwed if counterparty doesn't pay
		- Gaussian copula used here to model counterparty defaults
- Regulators are often quants
	- Actuarial quants
	- Regulators always win disputes
- Orientation
	- Product
	- Business (legal entity)
	- locational orientation
- Risk typically has a separate IT function
- Compliance
	- About regulatory expectations and following the law
- Reputation risk
	- Directly tied to stock price
	- Anything going to rep risk committee is very bad - usually an untrustworthy product
