# BIS
- **BIS** focuses on monetary policy, financial stability research, and banking regulation (e.g., Basel III).
- The bank for central banks
	- Works like a club of all central banks
	- Based in Basel
- Original intergovernmental regulatory entity
	- Bankers come together to decide how to regulate industry
		- Make suggestions to governments
- Within BIS
	- Financial stability board
	- IAIS (insurance supervisors)
	- IADI (deposit insurance)
- The point is to have general regulations across different jurisdictions
- Provides banking services to central banks, but i not accountable to any national jurisdiction
- Helped form the Basel Committte after Herstatt FX debacle
## Basel Committee
- Goals:
	- Make sure no institution escapes fair regulation by taking advantage of local laws
	- Promote uniform capital requirements to have a level playing field
- Core roles:
	- Exchanging information on developments in banking sector and financial markets to help identify current or emerging risks for global financial system
	- Sharing supervisory issues, approaches and techniques to promote common understanding and to improve cross-border cooperation
	- Establishing and promoting global standards for the regulation and supervision of banks, as well as guidelines and sound practices
	- Addressing regulatory and supervisory gaps that pose risks to stability
	- Monitoring the implementation of BCBS standards in member countries and beyond to encourage timely, consistent and effective implementation
	- Consulting with central banks and bank supervisors not members of BCBS to benefit from their input into policy process and promote implementation of standards, guidelines and sound practices beyond member countries
	- Coordinating and cooperating with other financial sector standard setters and international bodies, particularly those involved in promoting financial stability
