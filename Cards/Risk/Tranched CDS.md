# Tranched CDS
- Tranching a [[CDS Indecies]] by risk level

- The key idea is to slice the credit risk of a portfolio of reference entities into tranches based on the percentage of portfolio losses they absorb. Investors can then choose how much risk they want to take.
1. **Equity Tranche (First Loss)** – Takes the first losses up to a certain percentage (e.g., 0-3%). High-risk, high-reward.
2. **Mezzanine Tranche** – Absorbs losses after the equity tranche, usually between 3% and 7%. Moderate risk and return.
3. **Senior Tranche** – Absorbs losses only after the lower tranches are wiped out, often between 7% and 15%. Lower risk, lower return.
4. **Super Senior Tranche** – Rarely affected by losses, usually above 15%. Extremely low risk.
