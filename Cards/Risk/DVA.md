# Debt Valuation Adjustment (DVA)
- Positive adjustment
- Your own credit risk; represents gain if you default first (you don’t have to pay).
- Idea: what you have might be better than what others think you have, so you adjust for their bias against you
	- In a world where there was perfect information symmetry, your DVA would be what your counterparty drew up your [[CVA]] as
- Controversial and counterintuitive (e.g., valuation goes up when your credit quality worsens)
- Often thought of as the "value of the option to default"
$$\text{Fair value} = \text{Risk-neutral price } – CVA + DVA$$
