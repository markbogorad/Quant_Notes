# Credit Default Swaps
- Derivatives that allow one to either mitigate or take on [[Credit Risk]]
	- Most popular instrument to speculate on credit risk
- OTC
- Can be on a tradeable insurance contract against default or on an equity put option on an entities assets
- Usage
	- Express views on credit risk they might not have had not prior underlying exposure to 
		- Monetize the [[Marking to Market]] changes of credit risk
- **Traded at 0 NPV at inception**
- Most common events that trigger payment: bankruptcy and failure to pay
## Structure
- Seller is selling protection and buyer pays with periodic fixed income payments
- Long CDS
	- Paying coupons, get paid 1-r in event of default, don't pay face value
	- Determining the coupon you pay - CDS are price to 0 at sale
- Selling CDS
	- Essentially going long the underlying credit
## Pricing
- Part discounted PV of future cash flows
- Part probability of default
- **CDS spread:** not like other spread definitions, this is just the price it costs to buy the CDS
	- Ex: 300bps CDS spread means it costs 3% of notional per year to buy the CDS
