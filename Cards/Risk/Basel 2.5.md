# Basel 2.5
- Response to GFC - a mid crisis revamp done somewhat prematurely 
## Key Additions
- Added IRC - compensated for default and migration risk in credit
	- Stemmed from IDRC in [[Basel II]]
- CRM - correlation risk (contagion in credit world). 
	- Defaults cluster and they rarely occur independently 
	- Key metrics added:
		-  multiple defaults
		- spread risk
		- correlation vol
		- index-single name basis
		- index-bespoke portfolio basis
		- recovery vol
		- hedge slippage
- Stressed VaR added to capital computation
	- Goes through max history and finds worst spot - same computations depending on worst spot based on internal history
- Changes to standardized charges
	- accounting for re-securitization
## Capital Ratio
$$\frac{\text{Total Capital}}{\text{Credit RWAs + Market RWAs + OpRisk + IRC + Revised Securitization Framework + CRM + Stressed VaR}}$$
