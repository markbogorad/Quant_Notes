# Minimum Variance Hedge
- $\text{Minimum variance hedge (h)} = N_f / N_A$
	- Then you go short h * futures
- In a more formal sense:
	- $h* = \rho({\sigma_s}/{\sigma_f})$
		- ratio of variance * correl 
	- This is basically beta
- SQRT of R squared is correlation between 2 variables
