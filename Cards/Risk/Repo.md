# Repos
- Very short term (1 day - 2 months)
- Cheaper form of finance -> less regulated
- Separate legal framework
- Idea: borrow money overnight and use it to buy an asset, use the asset as collateral to secure loan
	- From liquidity providers POV - lend 100, get a security for 100
	- Usually a fixed income security
- Fully collateralized loan -> cheaper because you're sitting on a full guarantee
## Mechanics
- Seller (borrower): 
	- Gives an asset to buyer
- Buyer (Iender): 
	- Buyer returns cash now
- At maturity
	- Seller repurchases asset for a little more
	- Buyer gets cash back + money
## Negative Carry
- The **cost of funding** (what you pay in repo interest) **exceeds the yield** you earn on the asset you're financing.
- Paying more to finance the position than the asset is generating
