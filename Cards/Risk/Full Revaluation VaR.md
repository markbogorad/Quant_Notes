# Full Reevaluation VaR
- Simulating VaR under fully different market scenarios (instead of doing the Taylor)
- Choose a model
	- [[Historical Simulation VaR]]
	- [[Monte-Carlo Simulation VaR]]
	- [[Variance Covariance VaR]]
- Generate market scenarios
	- Either simulation or with past data
- **Fully revalue the portfolio in each scenario**
	- **Reprice every instrument**
- Construct a new PnL distribution
