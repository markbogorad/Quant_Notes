# Operational Risk
- Defined as the risk of loss resulting from inadequate or failed internal processes, people, and systems or from external events
	- Basically anything not falling under credit or market risk
	- Includes legal risk but excludes strategic and reputational risk (Basel II/III definition)
## 7 Basel Cases
- **Internal Fraud** – e.g., rogue trading, embezzlement.
- **External Fraud** – e.g., hacking, third-party theft.
- **Employment Practices & Workplace Safety** – e.g., discrimination, workers comp.
- **Clients, Products & Business Practices** – e.g., misselling, breach of fiduciary duties.
- **Damage to Physical Assets** – e.g., natural disasters.
- **Business Disruption & System Failures** – e.g., IT outages, cyber attacks.
- **Execution, Delivery & Process Management** – e.g., data entry errors, failed trades.
