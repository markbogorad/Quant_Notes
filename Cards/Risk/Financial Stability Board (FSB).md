# FSB
- **FSB** focuses on **systemic risk, financial regulations, and coordination of global reforms** (e.g., post-2008 crisis reforms).
- FSB promotes international stability by coordinating national financial authorities and international standard-setting bodies in developing regulatory, supervisory and other financial sector policies. It fosters a level playing field by encouraging coherent implementation across sectors and jurisdictions.
- Makes recommendations to countries basically
- Independent of the [[Bank of International Settlements (BIS)]] but hosted by the BIS
