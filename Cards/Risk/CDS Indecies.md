# CDS Indices
- Baskets of [[Credit Default Swaps (CDS)]] products
- Trading protection on a portfolio of companies
- At the event of a company default
	- Company is removed from the index, you're paid out % notional that the company was part of the index (ex: 125 companies and 1 defaults, you get paid 1/125th notional)
