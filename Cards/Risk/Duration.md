# Duration
Duration - change in price for a unit change in interest rates dP/dy
- Sensitivity of a bond to interest rates [[Interest Rate Risk]]
## Dollar Duration
- Price impact of a change in yield as a percentage of par (P and PV)
$$Duration_{Dollar}=-\frac{dP}{dy} = -\frac{dPV}{dy}$$
## Present Value Duration
- Most common duration method
- Price impact as a % of dollars invested (present value)
$$Duration_{PV}= \frac{Duration_{Dollar}}{Present Value} = -\frac{dPV/PV}{dy}=-\frac{dP/PV}{dy}$$
## Price Duration
- Price impact as a % of price
$$Duration_{price}= \frac{Duration_{Dollar}}{Price} = -\frac{dP/P}{dy}=-\frac{dPV/P}{dy}$$
## Portfolio Duration
- Taken as the weighted average of durations weighed by market value
