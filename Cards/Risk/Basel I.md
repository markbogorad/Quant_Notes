# Basel I  1988
- Key change: started considering [[Market Risk]] (the idea came into existence here)
### Standardized Approach vs Internal Models
- Gives companies the choice of standardized methods vs internal models
	- **Internal methods**
		- Did not necessarily get more complex - sometimes they would not be helpful
		- Justified if the company is big, its business is diverse, and has big economies of scale 
		- Need to justify internal methods to regulators
		- Internal methods must account for all key methods
	- **Standardized method** is for more linear businesses
	- Can mix both methods but discretion for this is important because you need to net methods for cumulative risk often
### Risk Treatment
- Equity risk
	- Splits market and idiosyncratic risk
	- Allowed governments to compare general risk across companies - can help them catch market dangers
## Capital Ratio
$$\frac{\text{Total Capital}}{\text{Credit RWAs + Market RWAs}}\geq 8 \%$$
- Market risk RWA = 12.5 * VaR * m + specific risk add on
	- m supervisory multiplier -> back then it was > 3
		- A fudge factors controlled by government based on reviewing the backtesting 
		- The larger the number, the higher risk (less trust on backtest from govt)
	- 12.5 corresponds to 1/8% -> scaled to prevent double counting
- Market risk was only equity and interest rate
