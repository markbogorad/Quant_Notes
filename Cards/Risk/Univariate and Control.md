# Univariate and Control Problems
 - **Univariate analysis** focus on a single variable at a time
	- Distribution? mean? Variance? Central tendency? -> looks to find overall behavior of a variable
	- Descriptive statistics and graphical analysis
- **Control problems**
- *Seeing the process over time* and checking if theres a normal predictable range
- Looking for natural causes and assignable causes
	- Natural causes -> inherent variations that just happen in the data randomly
	- Assignable causes -> happen for a particular reaosn
