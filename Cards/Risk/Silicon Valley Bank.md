# SVB Case
- March 9 2023
- A case of [[Liquidity Risk]] and [[Market Risk]]
- **Run on the bank, issue with fixed rate loans during hiking**
- Some number of US regional banks are apparently insolvent, if you compare the market values of their assets (**lots of fixed-rate loans and bonds that lost value as interest rates rose**) with their liabilities (deposits, etc.).
	- Got wrecked in a rate hiking cycle
	- Had a run on deposits
- **Problem with fixed rate loans: when rates rise, securities are worth less, can only be sold at a loss**
	- They were racking up a ton of unrealized [[Fixed Income MOC]] losses
	- Had to sell because depositors were requesting money back from global cash constraints
	- Incorrectly believed rising rates would be countered by their rising interest revenue
- Attracting new deposits became more expensive
- Their clients burned cash and equity financing was constrained
- Highly concentrated in VC tech startups (needed short term funding)
## Lessons
- Shadow banking will grow because regulation is getting stricter
- Bank runs more dangerous in social media age -> fear spreads quick
- Small banks suffer more from regulation -> everyone flooded to JPMC after SVB fell
