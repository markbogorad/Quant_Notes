# Expected Shortfall
- Captures low probability events in the full left tail -> takes the average of the entire tail
- Is a [[Coherence Measure]] because it includes subadditivity
- Weakness: needs good data
- Banks are reluctant to use this because they have bad data
- [[Bank of International Settlements (BIS)]] enforces a 97.5% ES -> almost equivalent to a 99% VaR
- Why its still not enough
	- Does not capture credit events which cause the biggest losses
