# SMA Approach
- Comparability at the expense of detail
- Part of [[FRTB (Basel IV)]] in 2016
$$ SMA Capital = BIC \times ILM$$
- Final [[Operational RWA]] is SMA Capital * 12.5

## Business Indicator Component (BIC)
- Bank income derived from business activity
## Loss Component (LC)
- 15X average annual oprisk losses over last 10 years
- Internal loss multiplier comes from here (ILM)
- As LC increases the ILM increases at a slower rate (with a constant BIC), for example a doubling of LC results in an increase of ILM of around 24 percent
$$ILM = \ln\left( e^1 - 1 + \left( \frac{\text{Loss Component}}{\text{BI Component}} \right)^{0.8} \right)$$
