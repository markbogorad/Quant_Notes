# Pre Basel I
- Published late 80s
- AKA Initial Capital Accord
- Tried to set a true international standard and level the playing field
- Large concentration on credit risk (on counterparty side mostly)
- 4 main goals
	- Set international standard (level playing field)
	- Risk weight by credit risk
	- Capture credit risk of [[Off Balance Sheet Risk]]
	- Define core vs supplementary capital
## Risk Weights
- Only applied to credit here

- Key restrictions
	- Tier II must not exceed Tier 1
	- Loan loss reserves = 1.25%
	- Subordinated debt can't be over 50% of Tier II

## Capital Ratio
$$\frac{\text{Total Capital}}{\text{Credit RWAs}}$$
- Required this be over 10%, as well as Tier 1 capital over 6%
