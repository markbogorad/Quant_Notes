# Hedging
- [[Minimum Variance Hedge]]
- Variance = Var A * Var B * 2 CovAB
- Hedging in equity markets
	- $N = \beta (V_A/V_F)$
	- Beta
		- $\beta_i=\frac{cov(R_i,R_m)}{var(R_m)}$
		- $\beta=\rho_{i,m} * \frac{\sigma_i}{sigma_m}$
