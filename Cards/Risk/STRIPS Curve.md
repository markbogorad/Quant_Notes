# STRIPS Curve
- Represents spot yields from [[STRIPS]] ZCBs
	- Will be higher than a regular [[Yield Curve]] that shows par yields (no loss in value from STRIPS by early coupon issuance, its the full piece at each STRIP)
## Uses
- **Generic Treasury Yield Curve**
    - Used for broad market analysis, economic indicators, and yield comparisons.
    - Often referenced in discussions about the economy (e.g., steepening, flattening, inversion).
- **STRIPS Yield Curve**
    
    - Used for **precise discounting** of individual cash flows in financial models, especially for derivatives pricing.
    - Preferred in **quantitative finance** when accuracy is critical, such as calculating forward rates or risk-neutral discount factors.
