# Toolkit
- Position size (90% of risk management is knowing what to own)
- Sensitivity analysis (greeks, stress tests)
	- Stress testing is like checking engine light
- VaR + ES
- Backtesting
- Risk/return analysis
	- On individual transaction level (term sheet describes transaction in detail)
- Common sense
- Decision authority
	- Hierarchy in risk approval (CRO, lower, etc)
- Concentration test (owning a certain % of futures is illegal)
	- Commodities often has people cornering the market
- Policies, procedures, standards (cyber risk)
- Planning process
	- Meet with desks to discuss their money making goals
- Moral suasion
	- "I dont think you should do that" vs "dont do that"
