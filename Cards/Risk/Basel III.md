# Basel III
### 5 Elements
1) Quality, consistency, and transparency of capital
	- Tier 1 must now be mostly common shares and R/E
	- No more tier 3 capital
2) Improve risk coverage
	- Added [[Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA)]] here
	- came abut with reversing risk neutral pricing
		- Risk neutral logic - discounted value of cashflows should correspond to true value of the security
		- Real world is not risk neutral because there is an implicit factor of default
		- CVA captures how much real life pricing is different from risk neutral pricing
3) Introduction of leverage ratios
4) Promote building capital buffers
5) Liquidity ratios
	- LCR
		- Outflows are not certain - need to model
	- NSFR
		- Long term version of NSFR basically - difference is liquidity horizon
