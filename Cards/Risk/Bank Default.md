# Default
- Failure to pay obligations to counterparties
- A firm is in default if it ALREADY has failed to meet obligations (missed interest payments or violated contractual terms on debt)
## Cash Flow Default
- When they've failed to meet obligations but because of some sort of friction (wrong currency, jurisdiction, etc)
	- Most typical is when they don't have cash in a correct currency
- Closely related to insolvency (slight legal differences)
## Balance Sheet Default
- Amount of assets is insufficient to cover debt outstanding, subject to [[Counterparty Credit Risk]]
