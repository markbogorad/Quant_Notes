# Explicit Euler
- $y_{n+1}=y_n+h⋅f(x_n,y_n)$
- **Key Features**: Uses the slope at the current point to estimate the next point.
- **Pros**: Simple to implement.
- **Cons**: Can be inaccurate for stiff equations or large step sizes.
