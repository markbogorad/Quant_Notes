# Entity-Relationship Model
- A way to design [[Relational Databases]] with "is a" or "has a" [[Inheritance]] style relationships

[![What is Entity Relationship Model in DBMS: ER Diagram - UseMyNotes](https://tse3.mm.bing.net/th?id=OIP.lIFpqKv0fdyVlXzfEqRCywHaFX&pid=Api)](https://usemynotes.com/er-diagram-entity-relationship-model-in-dbms/)


- "Is-A" -> triangle
- "Has-a" -> diamond
	- Unfilled diamond -> strong relationship
	- Filled diamond -> weak relationship (weak entity can't exist without being associated to the strong entity)
