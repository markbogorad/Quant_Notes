# OBDC 

> **ODBC (Open Database Connectivity)** is a **standard "translator"** that lets your app (like Python or Excel) **talk to any database**, even if they speak different "languages."

An **ODBC driver** is the specific translator that knows how to speak to **one particular database system** (like Hive, MySQL, or SQL Server).
