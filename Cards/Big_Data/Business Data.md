# Business Data
- Business data tells you what happened or is happening to those entities — like prices, volumes, earnings, ratings, corporate actions, etc.
	- Drives analytics, modeling, and decision-making
- `DailyTrades (Symbol, Date, Open, Close, Volume...)`
- `IntradayTrades (Symbol, Date, Timestamp, Open, Close...)`
- `Dividends`, `Earnings`, `CorporateActions`, etc.
## Global Business Data

|Feature|**Global Business Data**|**Asset-Specific Business Data**|
|---|---|---|
|**Scope**|Applies across **all asset classes**|Applies only to **specific types of securities**|
|**Examples of Assets**|Stocks, bonds, ETFs, derivatives — all types|Bonds only, funds only, structured products, etc.|
|**Examples of Data**|Trading prices, analyst ratings, issuer info|Coupon rates, call schedules, NAVs, clearing instructions|
|**Use Case**|Used in general analytics, dashboards, equity/fund comparisons|Used in valuation models, legal compliance, settlement|
