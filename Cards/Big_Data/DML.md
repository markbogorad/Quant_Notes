# DML – Data Manipulation Language

> Used to **manipulate the actual data** within tables (insert, update, delete, query)

|Common DML Commands|
|---|
|`SELECT` – Retrieve data|
|`INSERT` – Add new rows|
|`UPDATE` – Modify existing rows|
|`DELETE` – Remove rows|

DML operates on the contents of the tables, not their structure.
